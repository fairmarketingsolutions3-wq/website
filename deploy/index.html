Why we exist

The challenges we exist to solve

Tanzania’s agriculture sector holds enormous promise, yet five structural challenges continue to hold farmers back.

01

Accessibility & Affordability

Modern inputs and agri-solutions remain scarce and costly, placing them beyond the reach of grassroots farmers.

02

Awareness

Limited exposure to best practices keeps outdated methods in use, lowering yields and harming the environment.

03

Climate Change

Deforestation and shrinking water sources have disrupted rainfall, cutting yields and pushing food prices upward.

04

Urban Migration

Low rural incomes drive the young workforce to cities, draining the labour the sector depends upon.

05

Population Growth

Rapid population growth intensifies food demand, pressing the sector to produce more despite every obstacle.

Who we are

Founded on ethical, justifiable business

Fair Agro Enterprises Ltd was incorporated on 15th June 2022 under the Companies Act, 2002 of the United Republic of Tanzania, founded on the conviction that ethical and justifiable conduct of business is essential to progress the agriculture sector.

What began as a single integrated agribusiness in Iringa has grown into the parent company of five separately incorporated, strategically focused subsidiaries. Our ethos is unchanged: empower farmers across East Africa by making the best agricultural products, services and solutions affordable and accessible. People are our strength and partners for growth.

The Fair Agro Group

One parent. Five strategic subsidiaries.

Each separately incorporated, each focused, together an integrated agribusiness ecosystem reaching from import to the last mile.

Wholesale

Multi-sector B2B bulk distribution

The group’s wholesale engine, importing and supplying agricultural inputs, seeds and agri-technology in volume to franchisees, agro-dealers and resellers across Tanzania.

  • Supply backbone guaranteeing consistent, competitively priced stock to every Fair Input outlet
  • Open trade channel serving independent agro-dealers beyond the group’s own network
  • Partnerships with international seed and agri-tech companies

Role in the group: the procurement and distribution backbone of the entire network.

Retail

Franchise outlets for last-mile input access

Operates and franchises agricultural input shops, with a model designed for AMCOS, bringing genuine, affordable inputs and sound agronomic advice closer to rural farmers.

  • Established outlets in Iringa Town and Mafinga Town serving 1000+ smallholder farmers
  • A replicable AMCOS franchise format for co-operatives to run trusted input shops
  • Every outlet doubles as a knowledge point for best practices

Role in the group: the retail face of Fair Agro Enterprises and its direct link to grassroots farmers.

Propagation

Commercial seedling propagation

Produces healthy, uniform, professionally raised seedlings so farmers start every season with the strongest possible foundation.

  • Professional propagation reduces crop failure and shortens time to harvest
  • Improved varieties from reputable international and regional seed partners
  • Anchored by a purpose-built propagation facility

Role in the group: turns quality seed into quality seedlings for farmers and Fair Farm alike.

Production

High-value crop production & the TIPFA initiative

Runs the group’s farming operations, open-field cultivation of high-value horticultural and cash crops, alongside indoor production for controlled-environment crops.

  • Land assets in Iringa (~20 acres), Bagamoyo (~17 acres) and Arusha (~5,900 sqm)
  • Controlled-environment farming for consistent quality and year-round supply
  • Leads TIPFA, the Tanzania Indoor & Professional Farmers Association

Role in the group: proves the practices the group promotes, on Fair Agro Enterprises’ own fields.

Marketing

Branding, social media & marketing for growth

The group’s marketing and communications company, strengthening the brand presence of every subsidiary while offering the same services commercially to SMEs.

  • In-house brand engine driving the group’s visibility, content and campaigns
  • Social media, branding and marketing services for SMEs across the Southern Regions
  • Expanding its commercial client base towards nationwide coverage

Role in the group: amplifies every subsidiary, and helps Tanzanian SMEs scale alongside us.
