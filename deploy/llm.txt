# Fair Agro Group

> Fair Agro Enterprises Ltd (Fair Agro Group) is a Tanzanian agribusiness parent company, incorporated on 15 June 2022, that operates five separately incorporated subsidiaries. The group makes quality agricultural inputs, seedlings, produce and marketing services affordable and accessible to farmers across Tanzania and East Africa. Slogan: "One Group. Five Subsidiaries. One fair promise."

## About

- Legal name: Fair Agro Enterprises Ltd
- Type: Integrated agribusiness group (parent company)
- Founded: 15 June 2022, under the Companies Act 2002 of the United Republic of Tanzania
- Headquarters region: Iringa, Tanzania; regional offices in Dar es Salaam, Iringa and Arusha
- Reach: 1,000+ smallholder farmers served across Tanzania and East Africa
- Mission: Raise awareness of agricultural best practices by ethically and justifiably making products and services affordable and accessible to all farmers throughout East Africa.

## The five subsidiaries

- **Taifa Lote** — Multi-sector B2B bulk wholesale distribution of agricultural inputs, seeds and agri-technology; the group's procurement and distribution backbone.
- **Fair Input** — Franchise input-retail outlets (with an AMCOS franchise model) delivering genuine, affordable inputs and agronomic advice to rural farmers; outlets in Iringa Town and Mafinga Town.
- **Fair Miche** — Commercial seedling propagation producing healthy, uniform, professionally raised seedlings.
- **Fair Farm** — High-value crop production (open-field and controlled-environment) and leader of TIPFA, the Tanzania Indoor & Professional Farmers Association.
- **Fair Marketing Solutions** — Branding, social media and marketing services for the group and for SMEs across Tanzania.
